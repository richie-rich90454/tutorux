# Code of conduct

## Our commitment

We want Tutorux to be a welcoming project for tutors, developers, designers, researchers, and anyone improving education tooling. We commit to a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be direct and kind.
- Critique the work, not the person.
- Assume good intent while asking for evidence.
- Respect privacy: never post student data, recordings, transcripts, credentials, or workspace files.
- Accept constructive feedback and apologize when a mistake affects others.
- Help keep discussions focused on the project.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, intimidation, or discriminatory language.
- Sexualized language or imagery.
- Personal or political attacks.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Sustained disruption of issues, discussions, or pull requests.
- Retaliation against someone who reports a concern.

## Scope

This code applies in project spaces and when representing the project in public spaces.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainer through the repository profile or the security advisory form if the issue involves private data. Reports will be reviewed promptly and handled as confidentially as possible.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, close issues, reject contributions, or block accounts for behavior that violates this code. Enforcement decisions should be proportionate, documented privately, and focused on repairing harm.
